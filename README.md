# RELOC8

**Agentic AI for Relocation Intelligence**

Moving to a new city is overwhelming. You're juggling property sites, cost-of-living databases, neighbourhood reviews, transport maps, school ratings, and none of it talks to each other. RELOC8 fixes that. It takes everything that matters to you and outputs a single, ranked, explainable recommendation so you can make a confident decision without weeks of research.

## The Problem

Most people making a relocation decision end up with 15 open browser tabs, a spreadsheet they built themselves, and still no clear answer. Existing tools are built around one dimension: property portals show rent, cost-of-living sites show averages, neighbourhood guides give you vibes. None of them combine financial sustainability with personal lifestyle fit in a structured, explainable way.

The result is decision anxiety and expensive mistakes.

---

## How It Works

RELOC8 is a three-step process:

1. **Tell us about you.** Input your salary, monthly budget, household type, and personal priorities (safety, transport, schools, community, etc.)
2. **AI analysis.** The scoring engine evaluates 90+ neighbourhood data points against your criteria using a weighted algorithm.
3. **Get your scorecard.** You receive ranked area recommendations with transparent scores, financial projections, and a downloadable PDF report.

Every score is explainable. You can see exactly how affordability, safety, transport, and lifestyle factors were weighted for your specific situation.

---

## Features

- Transparent scoring — no black box, every recommendation is broken down by factor
- Rent-to-income ratio calculations and monthly budget projections
- Interactive map with neighbourhood-level data
- Community filters (mosques, churches, temples, schools, etc.)
- Household-aware recommendations for singles, couples, and families
- PDF report export
- Financial sustainability warnings (flags when rent exceeds healthy thresholds)

## Who It's For

RELOC8 is built for people making a real move with real money on the line:

- International students arriving in a new country
- Skilled migrants relocating for work
- Early career professionals moving cities for a job
