# RELOC8 Roadmap

## Year 1 — Build and validate

- Launch MVP in key UK student cities (Leicester, Manchester, Birmingham, London)
- Free model to grow the user base
- Target: 1,000 early users
- Onboard university pilot partnerships
- Continuously refine the scoring algorithm based on real usage data

## Year 2 — Monetise and expand

- Launch the token model (users start with 3 free prompts, purchase more for advanced features and reports)
- Expand UK coverage to 50+ cities
- Secure 10 university partnerships for bulk platform licences
- Add advanced financial planning tools
- Target: 10,000+ active users

## Year 3 — Go international

- North America rollout (Canada first, then US)
- Launch affiliate revenue partnerships with property platforms, relocation services, and utilities companies
- Corporate B2B2C pilots with large employers managing international relocations
- Mobile app release
- Multi-language support (French, Spanish, Arabic)

## Year 4 — Scale

- Predictive housing analytics (where is affordable now vs. in 3 years)
- 100+ global cities covered
- Full corporate partnership programme
- Advanced API for integration with HR platforms and relocation services companies

---

## Business Model

RELOC8 operates on a freemium model.

**Free tier:** Every user starts with enough tokens for 2-3 full neighbourhood analyses.

**Paid tier:** Purchase additional tokens for more searches, advanced filters, and PDF report exports.

**B2B:** Universities and employers can license the platform in bulk for their incoming students and relocating employees. This is the primary growth engine.

**Affiliate:** Revenue share with property platforms, utilities providers, and relocation services that we recommend through the platform.
